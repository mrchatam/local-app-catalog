# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute data about their
own country's apps without being talked down to. We pledge to make participation
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be specific and evidence-based. "This listing 404s, here is the URL" beats
  "this entry is wrong".
- Assume good faith about other countries and their apps. A catalog that spans
  the world will contain apps and services you have never heard of.
- Accept that a maintainer may ask for the country-scoped listing before marking
  something `verified`. That is the rule, not a judgement on you.
- Respect that data changes: an app being delisted next month is normal, and the
  nightly recheck demoting it is the system working.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, unwelcome sexual
attention, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of
discussions. Nationalistic dismissals of another country's catalog are not
acceptable here.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, and contributions
that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor
for behaviour they deem inappropriate. Report concerns to the maintainers via a
private channel on the repository (or an issue asking for one, if no private
channel is listed). Maintainers will review reports in good faith and keep the
reporter's identity confidential.
